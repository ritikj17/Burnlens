# Economics

Credex’s converted lead value depends on credit margin and repeat purchase behavior. A reasonable starting assumption: a qualified startup buys $25,000 of AI credits or discounted capacity over the first year. If Credex earns a 12% gross margin or spread, gross profit is $3,000. If 35% of buyers repeat or expand by another $15,000 at the same margin, expected expansion gross profit is $630. Estimated lead value after conversion: about **$3,630 gross profit**.

Funnel assumptions:

- 1,000 visitors
- 350 audit starts
- 220 completed audits
- 70 saved reports
- 25 high-savings leads
- 8 consultations booked
- 2 credit purchases

That means audit completed to consultation booked is 8 / 220 = 3.6%. Consultation booked to purchase is 2 / 8 = 25%. Audit completed to purchase is 0.9%. At $3,630 gross profit per purchase, 220 completed audits produce $7,260 expected gross profit, or about $33 per completed audit.

CAC by zero-budget channel is mostly time:

- Founder teardown replies on X: 4 hours for 20 audits, maybe 5 qualified leads. At $50/hour opportunity cost, CAC per qualified lead is $40.
- Community posts: 3 hours writing and responding, maybe 10 qualified leads. CAC per qualified lead is $15.
- Partner newsletter: 6 hours to coordinate, maybe 30 qualified leads. CAC per qualified lead is $10 plus relationship cost.
- Product Hunt/HN launch: 12 hours prep, highly variable, maybe 40 qualified leads if it works. CAC per qualified lead is $15.

For the tool to be profitable, the funnel does not need consumer-scale conversion. If a completed audit is worth $33 gross profit and the product costs under $2 per completed audit in LLM, hosting, email, and support, there is plenty of room for manual sales follow-up.

To drive $1M ARR-equivalent contribution in 18 months, Credex would need roughly $1M / $3,630 = 276 first-year equivalent purchases. At 0.9% audit-completed-to-purchase, that requires about 30,700 completed audits over 18 months, or 1,700/month. That is plausible only if BurnLens becomes a recurring benchmark asset, not just a one-time calculator. The product would need SEO around AI pricing, newsletter partnerships, shareable reports, and a credible database of anonymized spend benchmarks.
